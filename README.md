## 📌 1. What is This Project About? (The Basics)

In today's digital marketplace, thousands of customers leave feedback on products every single day. 

* **What is Customer Sentiment Analysis?** It is the computational process of analyzing text data (like customer reviews) to determine whether the emotional tone behind the words is **Positive**, **Negative**, or **Neutral**.
* **Why does it matter?** Businesses cannot manually read 28,000+ reviews to understand customer experience. Automated analytics transforms messy human feedback into quantitative metrics that drive business strategy.

## 🎯 2. Problem Statement & Business Solution

### 🚨 The Problem
A major retail brand experienced a spike in customer feedback across multiple platforms. Leadership lacked visibility into:
1. **Overall Satisfaction:** Is customer sentiment improving or declining?
2. **Operational Bottlenecks:** What specific areas (e.g., product quality, shipping delays, customer service) cause the most customer frustration?
3. **Actionable Prioritization:** Which issues need immediate intervention to protect brand revenue?
